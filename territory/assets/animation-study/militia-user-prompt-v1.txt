2x2 sprite sheet, transparent background, four walk-cycle frames of the same
chibi toy-style dwarf axeman from the reference: blue hood and tunic, brown beard,
silver shoulder pads, brown chunky boots, short stubby legs.
Camera: high front three-quarter view (about 35° above), character faces and walks
toward the lower-right of the screen. Face, chest and both shoulders visible.

Identical in all four frames: head, torso, arms, and axe are pixel-for-pixel the same
size and position. Axe held in the screen-left hand, blade pointing screen-left,
haft vertical. Only the legs change.

Short stride, small steps. Legs described by screen position:
"front leg" = the leg attached to the screen-left hip (closer to camera, drawn larger).
"back leg" = the leg attached to the screen-right hip (farther, drawn smaller, partly hidden).

Frame 1 (top-left), contact A: front leg steps forward to the lower-right, big boot
low on screen, heel down. Back leg trails behind to the upper-left, only its toe visible.
Frame 2 (top-right), passing A: front leg straight under the body, boot flat.
Back leg bent, its small boot lifted slightly, tucked behind the front shin.
Frame 3 (bottom-left), contact B: back leg steps forward to the lower-right — its boot
is SMALL and sits HIGHER on screen than the big boot in frame 1, peeking out from
behind. Front leg swings back toward the lower-left, big boot heel lifted, sole showing.
Frame 4 (bottom-right), passing B: back leg straight under the body, mostly hidden.
Front leg bent with knee raised, big boot lifted off the ground in front.

Frame 1 and frame 3 must have clearly different silhouettes: in frame 1 the biggest boot
is in front at lower-right; in frame 3 the biggest boot is behind at lower-left.

Each character centered with generous empty margin on all sides. No ground, no shadow,
no text.
